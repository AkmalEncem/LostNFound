Tips for a Good Report

📸
Add a photo

Reports with photos get 3× more responses than those without.

📍
Be specific about location

"Library Level 2, near the printer" is better than just "Library".

🎨
Describe unique features

Colour, brand, stickers, scratches, or contents help narrow it down.

📅
Report as soon as possible

The sooner you report, the better the chances of recovery.
