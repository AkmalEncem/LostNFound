About LostNFound

Bringing the UTHM Community Closer Together

A student-built web platform that makes it easy to report, search, and recover lost items across the UTHM campus — powered by community trust.

Our Mission

Born out of a real campus problem

It started with a lost student ID card before a final exam. No system to report it. No way to check if someone found it. Just a notice on a whiteboard that nobody reads.

We're Group 10 from BIW 20303 Web Architecture at UTHM. We saw that UTHM, like most campuses, had no centralized digital platform for lost and found. So we built one.

LostNFound uses community-driven reporting, real-time browsing, and safe in-platform contact to give students, faculty, and staff a simple and trustworthy way to reconnect with their belongings.

Features

What makes LostNFound work

Built with simplicity and trust at its core...

📋

Easy Reporting

Submit a lost or found report in under 2 minutes with a simple form, photo upload, and location pin.

🔍

Smart Search

Filter by category, campus location, date, and status. Find what you're looking for in seconds.

🔒

Privacy First

Your contact details are never exposed. All communication happens through our secure in-platform messaging.

📍

Location-Aware

Reports include specific campus locations — from G3 to the Library — making recovery faster.

✅

Claim Tracking

Mark items as Lost, Found, or Claimed. The community always knows the current status of every report.

✅

UTHM-Exclusive

Built for the UTHM community only. Log in with your UTHM email to ensure a trusted, verified user base.
